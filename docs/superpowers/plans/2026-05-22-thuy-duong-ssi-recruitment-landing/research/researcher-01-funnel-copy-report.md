# Research Report: Funnel / Copy Strategy

**Project:** Vietnamese personal-authority recruitment landing page for Trần Thị Thùy Dương, Giám đốc Tư vấn Chứng khoán 09 - SSI Hội sở, Thùy Dương Invest  
**Scope:** Conversion strategy, landing structure, CTA hierarchy, compliance wording  
**Constraint:** Use only prompt context + general marketing knowledge

## 1) Core Goal
- Primary goal: convert cold/warm traffic into qualified recruitment leads.
- Secondary goal: build trust fast through personal authority + institutional credibility.
- Tertiary goal: pre-frame the opportunity so users self-select before contact.

## 2) Target Personas

### Persona A: New investor / retail beginner
- Wants guidance, low intimidation, clear first step.
- Pain: confusion, fear of losing money, overload from market noise.
- Motivator: "có người dẫn đường", simple onboarding, trust in SSI brand.
- Message angle: an experienced advisor helps them start safely and understand the market.

### Persona B: Busy salary earner / HNWI-lite
- Has savings, wants structured investing but lacks time.
- Pain: no time to research, distrust of random tips, wants a professional channel.
- Motivator: convenient, credible, long-term wealth planning.
- Message angle: personalized consultation, disciplined process, no hype.

### Persona C: Relationship-driven warm lead
- Came via referral, social proof, or personal brand.
- Pain: needs reassurance and proof of legitimacy.
- Motivator: trust in the person, not just the product.
- Message angle: direct invitation from Thùy Dương, clear next step, human tone.

### Persona D: Potential recruit / collaborator
- Interested in opportunity, network, learning, or career path.
- Pain: unclear role, unclear support, uncertainty about earnings and stability.
- Motivator: mentorship, structured environment, professional brand.
- Message angle: "tìm hiểu cơ hội hợp tác / nghề tư vấn chứng khoán" without overpromising.

## 3) Offer Strategy

- The offer should not look like a generic "đăng ký tư vấn".
- Best framing: a **free, low-friction consultation / discovery call**.
- Positioning options:
  - "Nhận tư vấn đầu tư phù hợp mục tiêu cá nhân"
  - "Kết nối với đội ngũ tư vấn chứng khoán SSI"
  - "Tìm hiểu cơ hội đồng hành / hợp tác cùng Thùy Dương Invest"
- The offer should answer: why now, why this person, why this channel.
- Avoid turning the page into a full product brochure; keep it lead-gen first.

## 4) Recommended Landing Structure

### Above the fold
- Headline: personal authority + outcome + audience.
- Subheadline: SSI Hội sở + Thùy Dương Invest + what the visitor gets.
- One primary CTA.
- One secondary CTA for lower-intent visitors.
- Trust strip: SSI, title, years/experience only if verified later.

### Section 2: Problem framing
- Show common pain points:
  - không biết bắt đầu từ đâu
  - sợ rủi ro
  - thiếu người đồng hành
- This section should mirror the visitor's internal monologue.

### Section 3: Why trust Thùy Dương
- Personal authority narrative.
- Institutional legitimacy.
- Advisory style / philosophy.
- Social proof if available later.

### Section 4: What they receive
- 3-5 concrete outcomes:
  - đánh giá mục tiêu tài chính
  - định hướng cách tiếp cận phù hợp
  - hướng dẫn quy trình cơ bản
  - trao đổi 1:1
- Keep benefits concrete, not promotional.

### Section 5: Who it's for / not for
- For:
  - người mới
  - người có vốn nhàn rỗi nhưng chưa có kế hoạch
  - người muốn kênh tư vấn chính thống
- Not for:
  - người tìm "kèo nhanh"
  - người muốn cam kết lợi nhuận
  - người không sẵn sàng tìm hiểu cơ bản
- This section improves lead quality and reduces compliance risk.

### Section 6: CTA block
- Repeat the same primary CTA with shortened form.
- Add microcopy about response time and privacy.

### Footer trust / compliance
- Add disclaimer and contact details.

## 5) CTA Hierarchy

### Primary CTA
- "Đăng ký tư vấn"
- Best if the page is aimed at broad conversion.
- If the page is for recruitment-specific intent, use:
  - "Tìm hiểu cơ hội đồng hành"
  - "Đặt lịch trao đổi"

### Secondary CTA
- "Nhắn Zalo"
- "Xem thêm thông tin"
- "Nhận tư vấn trước"

### Tertiary CTA
- Scroll anchor CTA in nav.
- Sticky CTA on mobile.

### CTA rules
- One dominant CTA per screen.
- Use the same CTA verb across the page to reduce friction.
- Avoid competing CTAs like "follow", "watch video", "download guide" unless necessary.

## 6) Copy Strategy

- Tone: calm, expert, direct, feminine-credible, non-hype.
- Style: short sentences, clear benefit, no market jargon overload.
- Use identity copy:
  - "Trần Thị Thùy Dương"
  - "Giám đốc Tư vấn Chứng khoán 09 - SSI Hội sở"
  - "Thùy Dương Invest"
- Use reassurance copy:
  - "trao đổi trực tiếp"
  - "quy trình rõ ràng"
  - "phù hợp mục tiêu cá nhân"
- Avoid copy that sounds like sales funnels from consumer e-commerce.

## 7) Risk / Compliance Wording

- Do not promise profit, guaranteed returns, or "an toàn tuyệt đối".
- Do not imply certainty, insider access, or privileged stock picks.
- Avoid superlatives unless strictly verified:
  - "tốt nhất", "số 1", "cam kết sinh lời"
- Safer wording patterns:
  - "thông tin mang tính tham khảo"
  - "không phải khuyến nghị đầu tư cá nhân hóa" if needed
  - "kết quả phụ thuộc khẩu vị rủi ro, mục tiêu và thời điểm"
  - "vui lòng cân nhắc kỹ trước khi quyết định"
- If the landing is recruitment-oriented, distinguish clearly:
  - "cơ hội tìm hiểu hợp tác / nghề nghiệp"
  - not "đảm bảo thu nhập"
- Add a visible disclaimer in footer and under CTA if form collects leads.

## 8) Conversion Mechanics

- Reduce cognitive load:
  - one action
  - short form
  - immediate trust markers
- Increase commitment gradually:
  - value proposition
  - proof
  - low-friction action
  - follow-up channel
- Mobile-first matters because trust pages often get shared in chat apps.
- Use inline trust cues near CTA:
  - "Phản hồi trong giờ làm việc"
  - "Thông tin được bảo mật"

## 9) Unanswered Questions

- Is this page for **client acquisition**, **recruitment**, or **both**? Copy changes materially based on that.
- What verifiable proof assets exist:
  - years of experience
  - credentials
  - testimonials
  - event photos
  - SSI-approved brand assets
- What is the intended traffic source:
  - referral, Zalo, ads, Facebook profile, QR poster, or search?
- What is the expected conversion action:
  - form submit, Zalo chat, call, or calendar booking?
- Are there legal/compliance constraints from SSI that must be followed verbatim?
- Is there a preferred audience segment priority:
  - beginner investors, active traders, or potential recruits?

## 10) Bottom Line

- Best-performing angle is likely **personal authority + institutional trust + low-friction consultation**.
- The page should feel like a credible invitation from a real expert, not a generic lead form.
- Keep the CTA simple, the proof visible, and the compliance language conservative.
